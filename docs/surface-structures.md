# Surface structure placement

New natural surface starts retain their seeded structure-set owner and weights.
They evaluate the original site and eight adjacent chunk sites in a fixed order,
using each candidate's generated piece footprints and noise-only terrain queries.
Biome and generator checks run for each candidate. Ties keep the earlier site;
no additional structure slots are added. Unsafe sites can be rejected, so stricter
limits can lower the number of generated structures.

Each accepted start stores its selected site and immutable terrain plan in its
first saved child under `horizon:surface_plan`. Neighbour chunks share the cached
plan. Reloading restores the plan rather than running the site search again.
Existing starts without a plan keep their saved placement behavior; existing
chunk blocks are never regenerated by this feature.

The surface stage fits individual piece footprints, with footprint-scaled quintic
falloff. It runs after noise/aquifer filling and before normal surface materials.
Connected rigid building components settle together onto local ground; upper
storeys and basements retain their relative offsets. Terrain-matching roads use local graded planes. There is no single flat platform covering an entire village. Fill/cut
depths are bounded per column. Fluid columns, sea-level shorelines and thin cave
roofs are excluded. Deep cave cells and terrain beyond the blend radius are not
written. Fitted starts bypass the older uncapped density kernels to avoid applying
two competing terrain adaptations. Other structures keep their density kernels.

## Configuration

Per-world datapacks can override a surface structure's JSON in
`data/<namespace>/worldgen/structure/<name>.json`. Add this field to its normal
definition (including its existing biome/placement settings):

```json
"horizon:surface_placement": {
  "enabled": true,
  "max_slope": 1.0,
  "max_excavation": 8,
  "max_fill": 8,
  "radius_scale": 0.5,
  "min_radius": 4,
  "max_radius": 24
}
```

Slope is footprint relief divided by footprint span (at least twice the minimum
blend radius); road planes also limit their gradient magnitude. Depths and radii are blocks. Radius is the larger horizontal
piece dimension times `radius_scale`, clamped to the configured radius interval.
Depths accept 0–32, radii 1–32, and slope/scale 0–2. Invalid values fail registry
loading. `enabled: false` preserves original placement and density adaptation.
Defaults apply to surface-projected jigsaws (including villages and outposts),
desert pyramids, jungle temples, mansions and igloos. Underground, ocean and Nether
structures retain their specialized placement. `/place structure` keeps the
explicitly requested location and does not perform natural site searching.

Tectonic's native settings adapter includes `minecraft:beardifier` in its final
density graph, restoring the existing kernel pipeline for non-fitted structures.

Flat generators retain original placement. Saved plans retain their original limits;
configuration changes affect new starts. Waterfront pieces are penalized during site
selection and retain their original water footprint. Excavation beside fluids is
clamped to their level; edge queries use cached noise-only neighbour columns.
